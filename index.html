Beach recommendations

Two coastlines worth the flight, and an honest look at each.

Nyali Beach, Mombasa — Kenya

Wide, soft sand and water that stays warm most of the year. Nyali is the closest proper beach to Kampala — about 400 km, roughly six hours by road.

  • Best for: families, first-time visitors, long relaxed days
  • Best time: December to March and June to October
  • Honest note: the reef means strong currents at some beaches — ask locally which day is safe

Nyali has a marine park just offshore, so the snorkelling is the reason to come rather than the sand. Dhow trips run out at low tide, and the food is a serious reason not to skip lunch.

Nungwi Beach, Zanzibar — Tanzania

Turquoise water, a long shallow reef, and sunsets that pull a crowd. Nungwi is the beach most people picture when they hear "Zanzibar".

  • Best for: couples, photographers, calm shallow swimming
  • Best time: June to October and December to February
  • Honest note: Nungwi gets busy. For quiet, Kizimkazi or Paje are better and cheaper

Zanzibar is a short hop from the Kenyan coast, so many travellers combine the two trips. Stone Town is worth a full day — the old fort, the spice markets and the dhow builders' quarter are all free to walk.

Temple recommendations

Two places of worship worth planning a whole trip around.

Temple of the Tooth, Kandy — Sri Lanka

Sri Lanka's most sacred Buddhist temple, set beside an artificial lake with the city hills behind it. It is still an active place of worship, not a museum.

  • Best for: heritage, photography, anyone curious about Buddhist practice
  • Best time: December to March
  • Honest note: shoulders and knees must be covered, shoes come off at the gate, and the Esala procession in July/August makes Kandy extremely crowded

The Temple of the Tooth holds what Buddhists believe to be the Buddha's left canine tooth, and it is guarded without interruption. Go early in the morning: the rituals are most active then and the crowd is thinner.

Prambanan, Yogyakarta — Indonesia

The largest Hindu temple complex in Java, built in the 9th century from volcanic stone and still standing after earthquakes and centuries of weather.

  • Best for: architecture fans, history, combining with Borobudur
  • Best time: May to September (dry season)
  • Honest note: many reliefs have been worn smooth by visitors and are being conserved, so some detail you see in old photographs is no longer sharp

Prambanan sits about an hour from Yogyakarta and Borobudur, the world's largest Buddhist temple, is nearby. Buying a combined ticket for both is cheaper and lets you take your time across two days.
